Grammar: action or non-action?

Action verbs show actions: work, wait, build, weigh (= measure). They can be in the Present Continuous: They're working on the roof now.

Non-action verbs show feelings, thoughts and states. We usually use them only in the Present Simple, even when we talk about now.

Feelingslike, love, hate, want, need, prefer
Thinkingknow, understand, believe, remember, forget, mean
Havinghave (= own), own, belong
Otherseem, cost, depend

Verbs with two meanings

Some verbs can be action or non-action. It depends on the meaning.

VerbNon-action → only Present SimpleAction → Present Continuous is OK
haveиметь: I have a car / a headache / a day off.в выражениях: have lunch, a shower, a meeting, fun → I'm having lunch.
thinkсчитать, полагать: I think it's a good idea.думать, размышлять: I'm thinking about my holiday.
weighвесить: This beam weighs three tonnes.взвешивать: They're weighing the materials.
lookвыглядеть: You look tired.смотреть: I'm looking at the plans.
seeвидеть, понимать: I see the crane. I see what you mean.встречаться с кем-то: She's seeing the subcontractor today.
smellпахнуть: The paint smells strong.нюхать: Why are you smelling the paint?
tasteиметь вкус: This coffee tastes great.пробовать на вкус: He's tasting the soup.

consider (рассматривать, обдумывать) is an action verb: We're considering another plan.  feel (чувствовать себя): both are OK — I feel tired = I'm feeling tired.

Vocabulary · Review

Words from last time

Review the definitions. **Tap any card to reveal its Russian translation.**

to fall through
when a plan or a deal fails to happen.
e.g. Our party plans fell through at the last minute.
Перевод: сорваться, провалиться (о планах/сделках)
deal
a business agreement or arrangement.
e.g. We closed a profitable deal yesterday.
Перевод: сделка, соглашение
to list
to put items on a marketplace platform for sale.
e.g. She needs to list new items today.
Перевод: размещать товар на платформе
to handle
to manage, control, or deal with a situation or person.
e.g. He knows how to handle complex tasks.
Перевод: справляться, урегулировать, разруливать
offensive
rude, insulting, causing someone to feel hurt or angry.
e.g. The customer's review was quite offensive.
Перевод: оскорбительный, обидный
manner
the way in which something is done or someone behaves.
e.g. Everything was done in a polite manner.
Перевод: манера, способ поведения

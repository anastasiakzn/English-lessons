Warm-up: Business Phrase of the Day

To be snowed under
"After we launched the new wholesale campaign, our marketplace team was completely snowed under with orders."
Meaning: To have so much work or so many tasks that you have problems dealing with it all.
(Быть заваленным работой, по уши в делах)

Question for Discussion:

Have you ever been completely snowed under with work? How did you manage your time and stress during that period?

Reading Practice

A Stressful Renovation

Last month, we decided to organise a major renovation of our wholesale warehouse. We planned to expand the storage area. Right after the workers had painted the ceiling, a heavy storm started. Because we hadn't checked the old pipes, we suddenly noticed a massive water leak. I got so stressed that I developed a terrible migraine.

I went to the clinic, and the doctor said to me that I needed rest. He prescribed some strong medicine. When I arrived at the pharmacy, I realised that I had left the prescription at home! By the time I returned with the paper, the pharmacy had already closed.

I was so exhausted that I didn't want to cook anymore. I just went home, ordered in some pizza, and waited for the ceiling to dry.

Grammar

⭐ to be or an action verb?

⚠️ Two helpers never meet: am / is / are or do / does — but never both in one sentence.
Если в предложении есть смысловой глагол (live, play, feed), помощники — do и does. Если смыслового глагола нет, работает сам to be, и do / does ему не нужны. Вместе они не стоят никогда.

Reading

📖 Two friends, two cities

Read the text and do the two tasks below.

Прочитай текст и выполни два задания ниже.

Alisa is thirteen and she lives in Tbilisi. Her best friend Vika lives in St Petersburg, so they don't meet often. But they keep in touch every day: messages in the morning and a video call in the evening.

The girls have a lot in common. They both play the guitar, they both love old rock songs and they both have dogs. "Do you practise every day?" Vika asks. "It depends," says Alisa. "On school days I don't have time."

Alisa gets along with her little brother, but in the evening he is a problem: he wants to play games when she wants to call Vika. So Alisa and her brother take turns. First they play a game together, then she calls her friend.

At the weekend Alisa is a real couch potato: films, tea and a warm blanket. But she always looks after her dogs first — she feeds them and walks them in the park. And when Vika says, "Your new song sounds great!" — well, that's music to Alisa's ears.

Vika is never late for their calls. Alisa is late sometimes, but only for five minutes.

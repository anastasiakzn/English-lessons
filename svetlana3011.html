“A delay in processing can affect potassium measurements, so we should check the collection record before assuming that an unusual result reflects the patient's condition.”

delay in processing — extra time before a sample is analysed
to affect — to influence or change something
potassium measurement — a test result for potassium
collection record — information about when a sample was taken
to assume — to accept something as true without checking

4. Grammar: Past Perfect Continuous

Grammar Theory

We use the Past Perfect Continuous for an action that was going on for some time before another moment or action in the past.

1. How long before?When the courier arrived, the technicians had been checking the labels for 40 minutes.
2. The reason for a past resultShe was exhausted because she had been preparing her lecture all morning.
FormStructureExample
Positive (+)Subject + had been + V-ingShe had been working there for five years.
Negative (−)Subject + hadn't been + V-ingThey hadn't been waiting long when the bus arrived.
Question (?)Had + subject + been + V-ing?Had you been sleeping before I called?
Wh-questionHow long / Why + had + subject + been + V-ing?How long had he been learning English?
Short answersYes, I had. / No, I hadn't.— Had they been waiting? — Yes, they had.
Time wordsfor (two hours), since (6 a.m.), how long, when, before, by the time
ComparePast Perfect: She had packed 20 boxes. (result: how many?)Past Perfect Continuous: She had been packing for 3 hours. (duration)

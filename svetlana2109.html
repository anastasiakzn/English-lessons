4. Grammar: Third Conditional (Unreal Past Situations)

The Third Conditional talks about hypothetical situations in the past — actions that did not happen, and their imaginary past results (often expressing regret or historical analysis).

Condition (If-clause) Result (Main clause)
If + Past Perfect (had + V3) would have + V3
If the clinic had replaced outdated centrifuges... ...sample hemolysis would not have occurred.
Svetlana would have arrived in Tel Aviv earlier... ...if her connection flight had not been delayed.

*Notice that the result clause can also come first without a comma!

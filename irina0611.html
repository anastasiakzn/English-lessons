Reading Practice (2 Exercises)

The Holiday Tooth

💡 Read the story carefully. Then do the two exercises below.

Before she started her own business, Dasha worked as a cashier in a big supermarket. During the New Year holidays the shop was full of people, and the work was crazy. Customers bought junk food instead of healthy food: crisps, sweets and spicy meat sticks with pepper.

One evening an old acquaintance came to her till. "Do you remember me? We studied together!" he said. He was buying a huge bag of meat sticks. "This is my favourite entertainment," he laughed. "I watch political shows on TV and eat these." Then he took a big bite of a meat stick right at the till and broke his tooth!

The poor man ruined his holidays: he spent the next day at the dentist's. The dentist's fee was very high, and a month later the clinic raised its prices again. "If I weren't so impatient, I wouldn't have eaten it in the shop," he wrote to Dasha later.

This story gave Dasha an idea. She invented a soft meat snack that you can eat with a spoon, and now she sells it on a marketplace. Payments are secure, and buyers love it. "If that man hadn't broken his tooth, I would still be working as a cashier now," Dasha says. "I was fearless: I left my job and started my own business."

📖 A Sunday Morning Call

Read the story, then complete two activities. Pay attention to Present Continuous and the new vocabulary.

It's Sunday morning in Georgia, and Mia is getting ready for a video call with her sisters. Her phone is on the desk, but she isn't starting the call yet. She's finishing a sketch of the sunrise first. The sky in her drawing is pink and orange.

In St. Petersburg, Lily is taking a break from her studies. She's sitting near the window with a cup of tea. Their other sister is in London. She's packing a suitcase because she's travelling to Georgia on Tuesday.

At home, Mia's brother is turning down the music. “It's too loud,” he says. Mia is practising a new guitar chord for a short video, but she isn't recording anything yet.

A few minutes later, both sisters join the call. Mia is sharing her sunrise sketch with them. “We're all ears!” Lily says. “What song are you learning?” Mia smiles and plays a few notes. The sisters aren't in the same room, but they are sharing a special moment.

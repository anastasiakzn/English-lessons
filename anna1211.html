Grammar

Noun compounds and noun phrases

noun + nouna survey into the market → a market survey
number + nouna trip that lasts three days → a three-day trip (no -s, with a hyphen)
owner + 'sthe share of the market of the company → the company's market share
long phraseadverb + adjective / participle + noun + head noun: a highly confidential sales report, an expanding customer base

Personal passive

now / futureThe CEO is said to plan changes. The product is expected to launch in May.
in progressThe firm is believed to be negotiating with investors.
pastThe brand is thought to have lost customers last year.
It … thatIt is reported that the deal will be signed soon.

Verbs: say, believe, think, report, expect, know, consider, rumour.

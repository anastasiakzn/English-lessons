    Grammar: Past Simple

    We use the Past Simple for completed actions in the past — we know when it happened.

    Time words: yesterday, last week, last year, two days ago, five minutes ago, in 2020, when I was a child.

    Regular (+ -ed)Irregular
    + PositiveI worked yesterday.I went home.
    – NegativeI didn't work.I didn't go.
    ? QuestionDid you work?Did you go?

    Structures

    + Statement
    subject + verb (2nd form)
    I went home. · She worked yesterday.
    – Negative
    subject + didn't + verb (1st form)
    I didn't go home. · She didn't work.
    ? Yes/No question
    Did + subject + verb (1st form)?
    Did you go home? · Did she work?
    ? Wh- question
    question word + did + subject + verb (1st form)?
    Where did you go? · What did she do?
    After didn't and Did, we use the first form of the verb (no -ed, no “went”):
    ✓ I didn't go. Did you see it?   ✗ I didn't went. Did you saw it?

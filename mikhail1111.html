          Mathematics in Nature

          00:00 / 01:10

          Script: Good morning, everyone. Today, we will look at how mathematics can explain the beautiful patterns we see in nature. On the one hand, nature seems chaotic and unpredictable, full of random shapes and events. On the other hand, if you look closely, there is perfect order hidden everywhere. Take the Fibonacci sequence, for example. It is a fascinating series of numbers where each number is the sum of the two preceding ones. You can find this exact mathematical pattern in the arrangement of leaves on a stem, the flowering of an artichoke, and even in the spiral of a pinecone. For the first time in history, early scientists were able to make a plan for agricultural growth by observing and using this mathematical model. Understanding the difference between random growth and strict mathematical patterns allows us to predict natural events much more accurately. In many ways, mathematics is the secret language of the natural world. Thank you for listening.

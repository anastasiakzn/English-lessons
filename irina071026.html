To be under the weather
"Sorry, I'm a bit under the weather today."
Meaning: To feel a little ill or tired.
(Неважно себя чувствовать, приболеть)

Listening Practice (2 Exercises)

The Case of the Lost Candles

💡 Listen to the story about Kate, a marketplace seller, and her lost parcels.

Sentence 0 / 15
Last Friday, Kate sold two hundred scented candles in one day. She promised her buyers that the parcels would come on Monday. The weather forecast said there would be a big storm, but Kate decided to go the extra mile and drove to the state post office herself. On Monday, the buyers wrote that nothing had come. The parcels had disappeared! Kate was sure someone had stolen them, so she called the criminal investigation department. Two men came to her office. They asked a lot of questions, and the conversation lasted two hours. "If I were you, I would call the post office first," said the older detective. "If the parcels had been stolen, someone would have seen it. Let's continue tomorrow." The next morning, the post office called and solved the problem. Because of the storm, the parcels were still in the warehouse: the post office didn't allow the trucks to leave in bad weather. Kate laughed: "If I had listened to the weather forecast, I wouldn't have called the police!" Now, if a storm comes, she will wait. And the detectives? They bought ten candles each.

Practice: Conditionals (Types 1, 2, 3)

Grammar Theory

TypeWhen we use itIf-clauseMain clause
1stReal, possible situations in the present or future
Реальное условие		If + Present Simplewill + V
Example: If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.
(Если завтра пойдёт дождь, мы останемся дома — это вполне возможно.)
2ndUnreal or unlikely situations in the present or future
Нереальное условие в настоящем		If + Past Simple
(If I / he / she were…)		would + V
Example: If I had more money, I would open a second store.
(Если бы у меня было больше денег, я бы открыла второй магазин — но денег сейчас нет.)
3rdUnreal situations in the past; regrets
Нереальное условие в прошлом		If + Past Perfect
(had + V3)		would have + V3
Example: If I had checked the price, I wouldn't have lost money.
(Если бы я проверила цену, я бы не потеряла деньги — но я не проверила.)

💡 The if-clause can come first or second. Use a comma only when if comes first.

        IELTS Academic · ReadingShort original practice passage with two exam-style question types. Focus on skimming and scanning.

        Connecting visitors with a city museum

        Read the three short paragraphs A–C. You can refer back to the passage while answering.

        A The City Museum welcomes local families and international students. It opens at 10 a.m. every day except Monday. At the weekends, two guides lead short excursions through its European history collection. Visitors usually get to the museum by bus or tram. The PR team publishes information on the museum website and puts printed maps near the entrance.

        B This month the museum is trying a new approach to student visitors. It is working with a local university, and PR students are taking part in a four-week project. They are filming short videos about the exhibits and sharing travel directions online. The museum is keeping its normal ticket prices during the project. Each Thursday, the students meet the guides and discuss questions from visitors.

        C The university team is also studying how people plan their visits. Students are counting visitors at different times of day and collecting short written comments after excursions. The team is preparing a final presentation for the museum's communication manager. The museum is using the results to improve its online information, but the project does not cover changes to the permanent collection.

        Task 1 — matching headings

        Choose the correct heading (i–v) for each paragraph A–C. There are two extra headings.

Grammar

⭐ to be or an action verb?

⚠️ One helper per sentence: am / is / are or do / does — never both.
Есть смысловой глагол (play, know, sing) — значит, помощники do и does. Смыслового глагола нет — работает сам to be. Вместе они не стоят.

🎧 Dasha, Lera and the school concert

Listen to the story (about one minute) and do the two tasks.

Послушай историю (около одной минуты) и выполни два задания.
Hi! I am Dasha and I am fourteen. I play the guitar and my favourite band is Pink Floyd. My dad is a huge fan too, so I know their songs very well: I hear them in the car every weekend. Pink Floyd is a British rock band. It started in London in the sixties, so it is older than my dad! My guitar teacher says their songs are not easy, but they are perfect for slow practice. I practise twice a week, usually on Tuesday and Friday. My friend Lera is different. She is into pop music, and old rock is not her cup of tea. She says pop songs are catchy and easy to remember. But we have a lot in common: we both sing and we both play in the school concert. At the concert we take turns: first she sings her pop song, then I play my guitar. We keep in touch every evening and we never argue about music. My mum knows the lyrics of our songs by heart, because she listens to our practice every day.

Warm-up: Phrase of the Day

To be in hot water
"The courier lost three parcels. He's in hot water now!"
Meaning: To be in trouble, usually because you did something wrong.
(Попасть в переплёт, быть в беде, «влететь»)

Reading Practice (2 Exercises)

The Missing Signature

💡 Read the story carefully. Then do the two exercises below.

Mila sells handmade bags on a big marketplace. Last Monday she was under the weather, so she stayed at home with a cup of tea. Suddenly the marketplace informed her that a buyer had complained: the buyer said she had never received her bag.

An investigator from the marketplace called Mila. He said to her that the courier had delivered the parcel without the buyer's signature. "In these circumstances, we must check everything," he explained. "If the courier had taken a signature, there wouldn't have been any problem."

Mila was worried. Delivering a parcel without a signature isn't illegal, but the marketplace doesn't allow sellers to ignore complaints. If the investigation shows that the seller is guilty, there will be a punishment: a fine or even a blocked store.

Two days later the investigator called again. The courier had admitted his fault: he had left the bag at the wrong door, and a neighbour had kept it for a week! Mila regretted getting so nervous. "If I were you, I would combine two things next time: a signature and a photo of the parcel," the investigator said. Anyway, the buyer got her bag, and Mila got a five-star review.

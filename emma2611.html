Grammar · Present Continuous only

⏳ Four new grammar challenges

No theory today. Practise statements (+), negatives (−) and questions (?) in Present Continuous. The four uses are all included, even fixed future plans.

Vocabulary · 10 target words

🃏 Words in context

Try to explain each word in English before revealing its meaning. Tap 🔊 for pronunciation. No Russian translations.

agree
/əˈɡriː/
to have the same opinion as another person
I agree — that sounds like a good plan.
disagree
/ˌdɪsəˈɡriː/
to have a different opinion from another person
I disagree. The music is too loud.
quick
/kwɪk/
happening fast or taking a short time
We are having a quick break before rehearsal.
the whole
/ðə həʊl/
all of something, from beginning to end
She is playing the whole song.
according to
/əˈkɔːdɪŋ tə/
as someone or some source says
According to the message, we are meeting at six.
mood
/muːd/
the way someone feels at a particular time
I am in a creative mood today.
Give it a shot!
/ɡɪv ɪt ə ʃɒt/
a friendly way to tell someone to try something
A new guitar solo? Give it a shot!
rehearsal
/rɪˈhɜːsəl/
a practice session before a performance
The band is having a rehearsal on Friday.
temporary
/ˈtempərəri/
lasting for a short time, not permanent
This is a temporary place to stay.
stay
/steɪ/
to live or spend time in a place for a period
Nora is staying with her aunt this week.

Reading · A short story

📖 The Six O'Clock Rehearsal

Read the story and complete two tasks. Find details in the text; don't guess.

It's Friday afternoon. Nora is staying with her cousin Alex this week while her family is decorating their flat. Her visit is temporary. According to a message from their music teacher, the band's rehearsal is starting at six this evening.

Alex is in an energetic mood. He is practising the whole song on his guitar, but Nora isn't playing yet. She's making a quick drawing for the concert poster. “I'm not in the right mood for a difficult solo,” she says.

Alex is smiling. “I disagree — your solo sounds great. Give it a shot!” Nora is laughing. “Okay, I agree. But can you turn down the music? It’s very loud!”

At six, they're meeting their friends in the music room. Tomorrow morning, Nora is packing her suitcase because she and Alex are taking a short trip to the seaside on Sunday.

Speaking · Interactive mini-interview

🎤 The Creative Interview

Imagine you're interviewing a musician before a small concert. Keep your answers natural and short: 1–3 sentences each.

Part 1. You're the interviewer

Ask your teacher these questions. Listen, then ask one follow-up question of your own.

🎸 This week

What are you practising this week?

🗓️ Tomorrow

Are you meeting anyone for a rehearsal tomorrow?

🎶 Right now

What are you listening to at the moment?

🧳 This month

Are you staying in your hometown this month?

Part 2. Switch roles

Now you are the musician. Make up a simple situation and answer:

1. What are you getting ready for?

2. What are you not doing this week?

3. How are you feeling before your rehearsal?

4. Which song are you practising for the concert?

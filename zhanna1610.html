Two truths and a lie

Say three sentences about your life: two are true and one is a lie. Use usually / every day (Present Simple) and these days / this week (Present Continuous). Your teacher guesses the lie, then you swap!

Example:
1. I usually get up at six.  2. These days I'm learning to play the guitar.  3. I never drink coffee.
  • Something you usually do in the morning
  • Something you are doing these days
  • Something you never do

                    Ask: Are you going to the shop today?
                    Answer with a short answer and a full sentence: Yes, I am. I'm going to the shop at five. / No, I'm not. I'm staying at home.
                    At the end, tell us: Julia is going to the shop today. Masha isn't wearing anything blue.

                    В конце рассказываем: Julia is going to the shop today. Masha isn't wearing anything blue. Так тренируем и вопрос, и you are / I am, и she is.

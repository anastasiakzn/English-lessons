    Present Simple + adverbs of frequency. Rules first, then four exercises.

    Adverbs of frequency — how often?

      Before the main verb: I usually get up at seven. She never drinks coffee.
      After am / is / are: She is never late. We are often tired.
      Questions: Do you often go home? Is he always late?
      Negatives: I don't often watch TV.
      Also: every day · once a week · twice a month · three times a year — usually at the end: I go home twice a year.

                Task card

                Describe a typical day at your university.

                You should say:

                • what time you get up and how you get there
                • what lectures or classes you have
                • where you have lunch

                and explain which part of the day you like most and why.

                1:00

                Useful phrases

                • Start: I'd like to talk about a typical Tuesday. · On weekdays I usually…
                • Morning: I get up at… · I rarely have breakfast at home. · It takes me about … minutes to get there.
                • Classes: I study public relations. · My first lecture starts at… · We usually have three lectures a day.
                • Lunch: I usually have lunch in the canteen / in a café near the campus.
                • Why: I like the evening most because… · The best part of the day is…
                Use adverbs of frequency: always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never. Check the -s for he / she / it.

Reading Practice (2 Exercises)

The Spanish Order

💡 Read the story carefully. Then do the two exercises below.

Vika sells handmade ceramic mugs and pepper grinders on a marketplace. Her products are beautiful but very fragile, so she strictly follows one rule: every item must be wrapped in three layers of paper.

Last December was crazy. Vika had been packing orders since six in the morning when she got a strange message from a buyer in Spain. The buyer had ordered fifty mugs in advance for her café. She wrote in Spanish, and Vika doesn't speak Spanish fluently, so she was confused.

Vika had been trying to translate the message for twenty minutes when her son came in. "Mum, she wants the mugs with her café's logo!" he laughed. The problem was that Vika's printer had broken once again, and a brand new printer cost more than 300 euros.

Then Vika decided to think outside the box. She painted the logo on each mug by hand and charged two euros per mug for it. The buyer was delighted, and now she orders a hundred mugs every month.

Grammar: Past Perfect Continuous

Grammar Theory

We use the Past Perfect Continuous for an action that was going on for some time before another moment or action in the past.

1. How long before?
When her son came in, Vika had been translating the message for twenty minutes.
2. The reason for a past result
Her eyes were red because she had been crying.
FormStructureExample
Positive (+)Subject + had been + V-ingShe had been working there for five years.
Negative (−)Subject + hadn't been + V-ingThey hadn't been waiting long when the bus came.
Question (?)Had + subject + been + V-ing?Had you been sleeping before I called?
Wh-questionHow long / Why + had + subject + been + V-ing?How long had he been learning English?
Short answersYes, I had. / No, I hadn't.
Time words
for (two hours), since (6 a.m.), how long, when, before, by the time
Compare
Past Perfect: She had packed 20 boxes. (result: how many)
Past Perfect Continuous: She had been packing for 3 hours. (how long)

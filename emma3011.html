Grammar · Present Simple vs Present Continuous

⏳ Routine or happening now?

🔁 Present SimpleRoutines, habits, repeated actions

She plays the guitar every Saturday.

Does she play every week?

She doesn't play on Mondays.

often · usually · every day/week · on Fridays
⏱️ Present ContinuousActions happening now, temporary activities, fixed arrangements

She is playing right now.

Is she playing now?

She isn't playing today.

now · at the moment · this week · today · tomorrow (an arrangement)
Remember: He/she/it plays, but Does he play? / He doesn't play. For Continuous: am/is/are + verb-ing.

Reading · A short story

📖 The Art Club’s Busy Saturday

Read the story. Pay attention to what the club usually does and what its members are doing this week.

Every Saturday, the school art club meets at ten. Nina usually paints landscapes, and Oliver practises his guitar in the next room. They often take a quick break at noon. Their teacher asks everyone to listen attentively before each activity.

But this week is different. It is Friday afternoon, and the club is preparing for a charity exhibition on Saturday. At the moment, Nina is painting a sunrise, and Oliver is practising the whole song for the opening. Oliver is playing too loudly, so Nina asks him to turn down the volume. “I'm all ears,” Oliver says, and he turns the volume down.

According to the club's message, the students are meeting at nine tomorrow to get ready for the exhibition. Nina is packing her paints tonight, and Oliver is checking his guitar. They aren't taking a break right now because the rehearsal is starting soon.

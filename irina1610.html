Warm-up: Phrase of the Day

To have a lot on your plate
"Sorry, I can't meet today. I have a lot on my plate."
Meaning: To be very busy; to have many things to do.
(Быть загруженной делами, «дел по горло»)

Question for Discussion:

What do you have on your plate this week? What is the most important thing?

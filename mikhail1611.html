Present Perfect Simple or Past Simple?

Present Perfect SimplePast Simple
A result by now — and the result can still changeI have drunk two cups of coffee today.It is two o'clock — the day is not over, so I can drink more. If the result can't change any moreI drank two cups of coffee today.It is late evening — I will not drink any more coffee today.
There is a connection with nowI have lost my keys.I am still looking for them — I can't open the door now. There is no connection with nowI lost my keys.The story is finished: I found them.
Experience by now — and it can still changeI have been to Italy twice.My life is not finished: I can go again. Experience that can't change any moreWhen I was a child, I went to Italy twice.That period is finished.
News and new informationOur team has won the cup!You hear it for the first time. Details after the newsThey won the final on Saturday.We discuss when, where and how.

Signal words

Present PerfectPast Simple
just · already · yet · ever · never
today · this week · this month · this year		 yesterday · last week / month / year
two days ago · in 2020 · when I was a child · at seven o'clock
A finished time word like yesterday, last week or ago always wins: I have seen him yesterday ✗ → I saw him yesterday ✓.

The forms

Present Perfect
have / has + V3 · haven't / hasn't + V3 · Have / Has + subj + V3?I have seen it. She hasn't come. Have you finished?
Past Simple
V2 · didn't + V1 · Did + subj + V1?I saw it. She didn't come. Did you finish?

          The Light and Sound Pavilion

          Ready (approx. 1:15)

          Guide: Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to the City Science Museum. Today you are visiting our new Light and Sound Pavilion, which has just opened to the public this week. Five years ago, this building was an old printing factory, and for a long time it stood empty and boring. Two years ago, a group of physicists and modern artists asked the city council for permission to turn the space into an interactive science centre. In the first winter, the main hall was unavailable because the roof needed serious repairs, but after some time the team finished the construction.

          On the ground floor, you can look at optical illusions and see how mirrors change light. It is very inspiring, even for visitors who rarely read about physics. As for the second floor, it focuses on acoustics. We have built a special quiet room where you can sit on comfortable benches and listen to natural sounds from the mountains. Over the past three days, we have already welcomed four hundred visitors, and the children have not got bored for a single minute. Before we start our tour, please note that the café on the roof is closed today, so you can go into the courtyard to breathe fresh air and buy coffee there. Our first live experiment starts at three o'clock in Hall B.

                  Task card

                  Describe an interesting topic, law or discovery in physics or mathematics that you have learned about.

                  You should say:

                  • what this topic, law or discovery is
                  • when and how you learned about it for the first time
                  • how it works or where we can see it in real life

                  and explain why you find it interesting or inspiring for your future studies.

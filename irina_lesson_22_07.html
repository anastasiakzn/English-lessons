Grammar Theory & Practice: Modal Passive

Grammar Theory: Modal Verbs in Passive Voice

When expressing obligation, capability, or advice in the passive voice, use:
Modal Verb (must / should / can / have to / may) + BE + Past Participle (V3)

Type Structure Example
Positive (+) Modal + be + V3 The audit must be completed tomorrow.
Negative (-) Modal + not + be + V3 Information should not be shared openly.
Question (?) Modal + Subject + be + V3? Can this issue be resolved quickly?

Grammar: multi-word verbs

Unit 3 language review · Form, particle position and meaning.

1 · No object

Everyone turned up early.

The meeting went on for an hour.

Verb + particle; no object needed.

2 · Separable

We put off the meeting. / We put the meeting off.

We put it off. Not: put off it.

With object pronouns, place them between verb and particle.

3 · Inseparable

She looks after new singers.

She looks after them. Not: looks them after.

Verb + preposition stays together.

4 · Two particles

We get on with each other.

I’m looking forward to meeting her.

After the preposition “to”, use a noun or an -ing form.

Grammar

⭐ to be or an action verb?

⚠️ Remember: to be and an action verb never work together: I am play guitar ✗ · Do you be late? ✗
В утвердительном предложении с to be используем am / is / are. В отрицании добавляем not, а в вопросе ставим am / is / are перед подлежащим. Со смысловыми глаголами в отрицании и вопросе нужны don't / doesn't и начальная форма глагола.

to be — am / is / are

кто или какой: Emma is thirteen. · Her sisters are kind.
+Emma is thirteen.
−Emma isn't late. · They aren't at home.
?Is Emma late? · Are they at home?

action verbs — play, feed, live

что делает: Emma feeds the dogs.
+Emma plays. · They play.
−Emma doesn't play. · They don't play.
?Does Emma play? · Do they play?

Vocabulary

🃏 Words for family and free time

Tap a card to see the meaning and an example. Tap 🔊 to hear the phrase.

Нажми на карточку, чтобы увидеть значение и пример. Кнопка 🔊 — послушать произношение.
to feed
/fiːd/
to give food to a person or animal — кормить
Emma feeds the dogs before guitar practice.
a couch potato
/ˌkaʊtʃ pəˈteɪtəʊ/
a person who spends a lot of time sitting and watching TV — любитель лежать на диване
I turn into a couch potato on rainy Sundays.
to be late for
/tə biː leɪt fə/
to arrive after something starts — опоздать на
Don't be late for your English lesson.
to look after
/lʊk ˈɑːftə/
to take care of somebody or something — присматривать, заботиться
Emma looks after the dogs when her parents are busy.
to get along with
/ɡet əˈlɒŋ wɪð/
to have a friendly relationship — ладить с кем-то
Emma gets along with her sisters.
to keep in touch
/kiːp ɪn tʌtʃ/
to continue communicating — поддерживать связь
They keep in touch by phone.
to practise the guitar
/ˈpræktɪs ðə ɡɪˈtɑː/
to play regularly in order to improve — заниматься игрой на гитаре
She practises the guitar after school.
to take turns
/teɪk tɜːnz/
to do something one after another — делать что-то по очереди
The sisters take turns choosing a film.
to have something in common
/hæv ˈsʌmθɪŋ ɪn ˈkɒmən/
to share an interest or quality — иметь что-то общее
They all love music, so they have something in common.
to miss somebody
/mɪs ˈsʌmbədi/
to feel sad because somebody is far away — скучать по кому-то
Emma misses her brother when he is in Austria.

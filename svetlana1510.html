1. Warm-up

"Diagnostic accuracy depends not only on the analytical performance of the analyzer, but also on how the specimen was collected, stored and transported to the laboratory."

• diagnostic accuracy — how well a test shows the real condition of the patient
• analytical performance — how precisely and correctly an analyzer measures
• specimen — a sample taken for testing
• to store — to keep something somewhere until it is needed
• to depend on — to be influenced or decided by something
«Диагностическая точность зависит не только от аналитических характеристик анализатора, но и от того, как образец был взят, хранился и доставлялся в лабораторию». diagnostic accuracy — диагностическая точность; analytical performance — аналитические характеристики; specimen — образец; to store — хранить; to depend on — зависеть от.

Discussion Questions:

  • In your experience, which stage causes more errors: collection, storage or transport? Why?
  • How do you explain to students the difference between analytical and pre-analytical errors?
  • Which part of this process is the easiest to improve in a regional clinic?

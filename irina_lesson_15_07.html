Exercise 2: Rewrite in Present Perfect Passive

Focus on the result. Convert the Active sentences to Passive.

  • 1. Active: We have definitely modified all the incorrect listings.
    → Passive: All the incorrect listings...

    have definitely been modified

  • 2. Active: The system has automatically sent the missing documents to the agency.
    → Passive: The missing documents...

    have been automatically sent to the agency

  • 3. Active: Nobody has warned us about the massive traffic jams in the pedestrian area.
    → Passive: We...

    haven't been warned about the massive traffic jams in the pedestrian area

  • 4. Active: The city has officially opened a beautiful new pedestrian area near the mosque.
    → Passive: A beautiful new pedestrian area...

    has been officially opened near the mosque

  • 5. Active: Have they finally resolved the tough relocation problems?
    → Passive: Have...

    the tough relocation problems finally been resolved

  • 6. Active: Someone has fed the rabbit for the second time today.
    → Passive: The rabbit...

  • 7. Active: The manager hasn't modified our upcoming dynamic schedule yet.

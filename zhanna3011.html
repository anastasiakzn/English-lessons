Grammar: Past Continuous

Past Continuous — quick reminder

1. Action in progress at a specific moment in the past

At 7 p.m. yesterday, I was discussing our project.

When the teacher called, we were working on the website.

A specific time or another event in Past Simple identifies the moment.

2. Two actions in progress at the same time

While Mia was taking photos, Ben was speaking to the guide.

Both processes were happening at the same time.

Remember: was / were + verb-ing. For an action in progress, at 5 o'clock yesterday is more precise than yesterday alone.

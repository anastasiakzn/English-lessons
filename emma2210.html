Listening

🎧 Oleg's Sunday

Listen to the story (about one minute) and do the two tasks.

Послушай историю (около одной минуты) и выполни два задания.
00:00
Hi! My name is Oleg and I am twelve. I love Sunday, because on Sunday I am not an early bird at all. On school days I wake up at seven o'clock, but at the weekend I sleep like a log until half past ten. My dog Rocky is different. He gets up at six every day and he waits at my door. On Sunday my dad walks him in the park, because I am still in bed. At eleven o'clock we have breakfast together. My mum makes pancakes and we eat a lot of them — pancakes are our Sunday tradition. After breakfast I practise the guitar for an hour, and then my friends and I play football in the yard. In the evening I watch a film with my sister and I usually fall asleep on the sofa. My dad says: "Hey, night owl, go to bed!" I don't like Monday mornings, but I love my Sundays.

Homework · Grammar

⭐ am / is / are

Exercise 1. Put in the right form of to be

Look at the sign: + statement, − negative, ? question.

Смотри на значок: + утверждение, − отрицание, ? вопрос.
  1. +My brother at school today.
    My brother is at school today.
  2. −We in the garden now.
    We are not (aren't) in the garden now.
  3. ? you over the moon today?
    Are you over the moon today?
  4. +My laptop on the table.
    My laptop is on the table.
  5. −I lazy on Mondays.
    I am not (I'm not) lazy on Mondays.
  6. ? your dogs friendly with cats?
    Are your dogs friendly with cats?

Exercise 2. Make a sentence from the words

Put the words in the right order. The sign tells you what to make: + statement, − negative, ? question.

Составь предложение из слов. Значок подсказывает, что нужно получить: + утверждение, − отрицание, ? вопрос.
  1. +online / is / my / school
    My school is online.
  2. −heavy / my / backpack / not / is
    My backpack is not (isn't) heavy.
  3. ?ready / your / backpack / is
    Is your backpack ready?
  4. +are / cheerful / classmates / my
    My classmates are cheerful.
  5. −in the garage / the tent / not / is
    The tent is not (isn't) in the garage.
  6. ?a big adventure / the new school year / is
    Is the new school year a big adventure?

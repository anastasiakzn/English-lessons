Warm-up: Idiom of the Day

To play it by ear
"We don't have a strict plan for today, so let's just play it by ear."
Meaning: To decide how to deal with a situation as it develops rather than following a plan.
(Действовать по ситуации, импровизировать по ходу дела)

Question for Discussion:

Do you prefer to plan everything in advance in business, or do you often play it by ear?

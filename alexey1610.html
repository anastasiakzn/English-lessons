Grammar: Present Simple or Present Continuous?

Ask yourself one question: is it a habit, or is it happening now / for a short time?

A habit, a routine, a fact?

Present Simple

He works six days a week.
He doesn't work on Sundays.
Does he work at weekends?

usually, always, often, sometimes, never, every day, twice a week, on Saturdays

Now, or only for a short time?

Present Continuous

He is working from home today.
He isn't working this week.
Is he working now?

now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, these days, Look! Listen!

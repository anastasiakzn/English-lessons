      Short answers

      Do you / they …?Yes, I do. · No, they don't.
      Does he / she / it …?Yes, she does. · No, he doesn't.
      Are you / they …?Yes, I am. · No, they aren't.
      Is he / she / it …?Yes, it is. · No, she isn't.

      Adverbs of frequency

        Before the main verb: I usually have lunch at one. · After am / is / are: She is often late.

                IELTS Speaking · Part 21 minute to prepare and make notes, then speak for 1–2 minutes.

                Task card

                Describe a place where you like to have lunch.

                You should say:

                • where it is
                • what you usually have there
                • who you go there with

                and explain why you like this place.

                1:00

                Useful phrases

                • Start: I'd like to talk about… · It's a small café / the university canteen.
                • Where: It's near my university / in the city centre / five minutes from my flat.
                • Food: I usually have soup and a salad for lunch there. · They make great coffee.
                • Who with: I usually go there with my groupmates / alone.
                • Why: I like it because it's cheap / cosy / quiet. · The staff are friendly.
                Remember: have lunch, have soup for lunch — no the, and use for.

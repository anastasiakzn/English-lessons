1. Warm-up: Phrase of the Day

A professional phrase for presenting your poster:

"Let me walk you through our findings."

"Good afternoon! Let me walk you through our findings — we compared serum tubes from two manufacturers under the same conditions."

Meaning: to explain something to someone step by step, from the beginning to the end. It sounds polite, confident and professional.
Similar phrases: "Let me briefly explain our main results.", "I'll take you through the key points."
«Позвольте, я коротко расскажу о наших результатах / проведу вас по основным результатам».

Discussion Questions:

  • Imagine a visitor stops at your poster. Walk them through your findings in two minutes.
  • Which part of your study is the hardest to explain in English? Why?
  • At a conference, how do you usually start a conversation with someone you don't know?

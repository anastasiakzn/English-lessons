1. Warm-up: Phrase of the Day

A professional phrase for talking to colleagues at a conference:

"I'd appreciate your input on…"

"Dr Rao, I'd appreciate your input on our centrifugation protocol. We've had some problems with sample quality."

Meaning: a polite, professional way to ask an expert for their opinion, comments or advice. Input = ideas, comments or professional advice.
Similar phrases: "I'd value your opinion on…", "Could I ask for your perspective on…?", "I'd be grateful for any feedback on…"
«Я была бы признательна за ваше мнение / ваши комментарии по поводу…»

Discussion Questions:

  • Whose input would you appreciate most at the conference in India? What would you ask them?
  • When did a colleague last ask for your input? What was it about?
  • What three questions do you expect visitors to ask about your poster?

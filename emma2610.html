Grammar

⭐ to be or an action verb?

⚠️ Remember: these two never work together. I am get up ✗ · Do you be late? ✗
Глагол to be и смысловой глагол не стоят вместе. Если в предложении есть смысловой глагол (get up, feed, play), помощники — do / does. Если смыслового глагола нет, работает сам to be — и do / does ему не нужны.

to be — am / is / are

кто или какой: Emma is twelve. I am late.
+I am a night owl. · She is late.
−I am not · She isn't · We aren't
?Am I late? Is she late? Are we late?
↪Yes, I am. / No, she isn't.

action verbs — get up, feed, play

что делает: Emma feeds the dog.
+I get up at seven. · He gets up at six.
−I don't get up · He doesn't get up
?Do you get up? Does he get up?
↪Yes, I do. / No, he doesn't.

Reading

📖 Yana and her alarm clock

Read the text and do the two tasks below.

Прочитай текст и выполни два задания ниже.

1Hi! My name is Yana, I am twelve and I am a real night owl. I love quiet evenings and I am never tired at eleven o'clock. My mum says: "You are not a teenager, you are an owl!"

2Mornings are not a pleasure for me. My alarm clock rings at seven, but I don't get up. My mum wakes me up twice, and sometimes three times. I wake up at half past seven, so I have only half an hour for breakfast and school. I am often late for my first lesson.

3Our dog Tyson is different. He doesn't need an alarm clock: he gets up at six every day. At seven he takes his bowl and makes a loud sound at my door. It is his way to say: "Feed me!" My dad usually feeds him, but at the weekend it is my job.

4In the evening I do my homework for an hour, and then I watch a film with my sister. I hit the sack at about eleven. On Sunday nobody wakes me up and I sleep until eleven. Sunday is my favourite day of the week.

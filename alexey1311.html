Grammar: Past Simple

We use the Past Simple for finished actions at a finished time in the past.

Signal words: yesterday, last Monday / week / year, two days ago, in 2015, at eleven o'clock, then.

+ STATEMENTsubject + 2nd formThe excavator dug the pit.
We asked him for help.
− NEGATIVEsubject + didn't + 1st formThe cable didn't belong to us.
They didn't find the plan.
? QUESTION(question word) + did + subject + 1st formDid you see the cable?
What did the bucket damage?

After did and didn't the verb goes back to the 1st form: didn't dig, not didn't dug.  Did you find it?, not Did you found it?

Regular verbs: + ed

RuleExamples
most verbs: + edask → asked  ·  damage → damaged  ·  search → searched
consonant + y → iedcarry → carried  ·  try → tried  ·  study → studied
short verb, one vowel: double the consonantstop → stopped  ·  plan → planned

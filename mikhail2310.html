    When do we use the Past Simple?

    • A finished action at a finished time in the past Indicators: yesterday · last week / month / summer / year · two days ago · in 2020 · when I was a child I bought a bike last summer. We went to the lake two days ago.
    • A story: one action after another I got up, had breakfast and rode to the park.
    Last summer, yesterday, ago → Past Simple. Not We have gone to Sochi last summer ✗ → We went to Sochi last summer. ✓

    To be: was / were

    + affirmative
    I / He / She / It wasThe museum was closed.
    We / You / They wereWe were very tired.
    − negative
    was not (wasn't) / were not (weren't)I wasn't at home. They weren't at the lecture.
    ? question
    Was / Were + subject …?Was the exhibition interesting? Were you at the lake?

    Other verbs

    + affirmative
    all persons + V2 (V-ed or irregular)I watched a film. She rode 30 km.
    − negative
    all persons + didn't + V1We didn't go to the museum.
    ? question
    Did + subject + V1 …?Did you see the new car?
    Wh- + did + subject + V1 …?Where did you go last weekend?
    Didn't / did → V1. He didn't went ✗ → He didn't go ✓. Did you saw ✗ → Did you see ✓.
    Never was + verb. I was spended ✗ → I spent ✓. Was / were go with adjectives, nouns and places: I was tired. I was at home.

    Spelling: -ed

    RuleExamples
    most verbs + edwork → worked, watch → watched
    verb ends in -e + dlive → lived, decide → decided
    consonant + y → iedstudy → studied, try → tried (but play → played)
    short verb: vowel + consonant → doublestop → stopped, plan → planned

    Irregular verbs: three forms

    For the Past Simple we need V2. V3 is for later tenses (Present Perfect). Type a verb to find it.

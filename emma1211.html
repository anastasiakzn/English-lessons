Grammar

⏳ Present Continuous

When do we use Present Continuous?
1. Unfinished actions which are happening right now.
Незаконченные действия прямо сейчас.		 I am playing the guitar now.
2. Unfinished actions around the moment of speaking.
Действия в процессе в текущий период.		 I am learning a new track these days.
3. Temporary situations.
Временные ситуации.		 My sister is living in London this year.
4. Plans which we are sure about.
Запланированные действия (100%).		 We are flying to Austria tomorrow.
🕰 Indicators: now, right now, at the moment, today, these days, this week/year.

⚠️ Structure:
+ Subject + am / is / are + verbing
− Subject + am / is / are + not + verbing
? Question word + am / is / are + Subject + verbing?

✍️ Spelling rules:
• play → playing
• make → making (немая -e убегает)
• sit → sitting (удвоение согласной после краткой гласной)
• lie → lying (ie меняется на y)

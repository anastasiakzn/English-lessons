Speaking

Describe a colleague you like working with.

You should say:

  • who this person is and what his or her job is
  • what he or she usually does at work
  • what he or she is working on these days

and explain why you like working with this person.

This is like Part 2 of the IELTS Speaking test. One minute to prepare (write key words only), then speak for 1–2 minutes.

Present Perfect Simple or Past Simple?

Present Perfect SimplePast Simple
A result by now — and the result can still changeI have drunk two cups of coffee today.It is two o'clock — the day is not over, so I can drink more. If the result can't change any moreI drank two cups of coffee today.It is late evening — I will not drink any more coffee today.
There is a connection with nowI have lost my keys.I am still looking for them — I can't open the door now. There is no connection with nowI lost my keys.The story is finished: I found them.
Experience by now — and it can still changeI have been to Italy twice.My life is not finished: I can go again. Experience that can't change any moreWhen I was a child, I went to Italy twice.That period is finished.
News and new informationOur team has won the cup!You hear it for the first time. Details after the newsThey won the final on Saturday. Nina scored two goals.We discuss when, where and how.

Signal words

Present PerfectPast Simple
just · already · yet · ever · never
today · this week · this year		 yesterday · last week / month / year
two days ago · in 2020 · when I was a child · at seven o'clock
A time word like yesterday or ago always wins: I have lost my keys yesterday ✗ → I lost my keys yesterday ✓.

The forms

Present Perfect
have / has + V3I have seen it. She hasn't come. Have you finished?
Past Simple
V2 · didn't + V1 · Did + subject + V1?I saw it. She didn't come. Did you finish?

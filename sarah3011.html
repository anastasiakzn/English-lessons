Reminder · action and non-action verbs

Action verbs show actions: work, watch, cook, run. They can be in the Present Continuous: I'm watching a TV series now.
Non-action verbs show feelings, thoughts and states. We usually use them only in the Present Simple, even when we talk about now.

Feelings

like, love, hate, want, need, prefer

Thinking

know, understand, believe, remember, forget, mean

Having

have (= own), own, belong

Other

seem, cost, depend

✓ Correct✕ Wrong
I want a coffee now.I'm wanting a coffee now.
She doesn't understand the plot.She isn't understanding the plot.
Do you know this number?Are you knowing this number?
It depends on the weather.It's depending on the weather.

Verbs with two meanings

Some verbs can be action or non-action. It depends on the meaning.

VerbNon-action → only Present SimpleAction → Present Continuous is OK
haveиметь: I have a car / a headache / a day off.in phrases: have lunch, a shower, a meeting, fun → I'm having lunch.
thinkсчитать, полагать: I think it's a good idea.думать, размышлять: I'm thinking about my holiday.
tasteиметь вкус: This soup tastes great.пробовать на вкус: The chef is tasting the soup.
smellпахнуть: The flowers smell nice.нюхать: Why are you smelling the milk?
weighвесить: My bag weighs 5 kilos.взвешивать: The shop assistant is weighing the apples.
lookвыглядеть: You look tired.смотреть: I'm looking at the photos.
seeвидеть / понимать: I see a bird. I see what you mean.встречаться с кем-то: She's seeing a client at the moment.
feel (чувствовать себя): both are OK — I feel tired = I'm feeling tired.

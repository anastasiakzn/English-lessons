IELTS Speaking Part 2 · One minute to prepare, then speak for 1–2 minutes. Use Past Simple to describe your experience.

Describe a film or cartoon you watched and enjoyed.

You should say:

  • what you watched and when
  • who you watched it with
  • what happened in the story

and explain why you enjoyed it.

Speaking

Describe a change in your life that took you a long time to get used to.

You should say:

  • what the change was and when it happened
  • what you used to do before it
  • what was the hardest thing to get used to

and explain how you feel about the change now.

Use used to, get used to and at least one participle clause (Having…, Not knowing…).

    Past Simple · quick reminder

    • A finished action at a finished time in the pastyesterday · last week / month / summer · two days ago · in 2020We went to a concert last Friday. She bought a foreign passport cover yesterday.
    • A story: one action after anotherI met the composer, heard her new piece and took a photo.
    Affirmative: subject + V2 → They saw a giant sculpture.
    Negative: subject + didn't + V1 → They didn't see it.
    Question: Did + subject + V1? → Did they see it?
    To be: was / were · wasn't / weren't · Was / Were + subject?

    Irregular verbs to look at

    go → wentsee → sawbuy → boughtbring → broughtthink → thoughtfind → foundhear → heardmeet → mettake → tookgive → gavecome → cameread → read (sounds “red”)write → wroteride → rode
    Remember: after did / didn't, use V1: didn't went ✗ → didn't go ✓; Did you saw? ✗ → Did you see? ✓.

            A piano with a second life

            A short community-news feature

            Composer Nina Volkova found an old piano in her building’s shared hall. It had belonged to a music teacher who left the city ten years earlier. One key did not work, so Nina decided to repair it.

            Every Saturday for a month, Nina carried her tools downstairs. Her neighbour Pavel, who usually stayed at home at weekends, came to help. His grandmother taught him to play the piano. They cleaned the keys, replaced two parts and tuned it. Worried that it might still sound bad, they invited a technician, who said it was in good condition.

            In June, residents organised an outdoor music afternoon in the courtyard. Nina played a piece by a famous composer, and Pavel sang with a local choir. Around sixty neighbours came. A giant paper moon hung above the piano. One woman had a foreign passport in her bag for a trip the next morning, but stayed for the whole concert.

            Afterwards, people called friends and shared photos. The manager said everyone should be proud of their work. The piano is back in the hall and gives neighbours a reason to meet.

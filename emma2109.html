Grammar

⭐ The verb to be: am / is / are

PersonFull formShort formExample
IamI'mI am twelve.
youareyou'reYou are my teacher.
he / she / itishe's / she's / it'sMy brother is at school.
wearewe'reWe are a big family.
theyarethey'reMy dogs are friendly.

+ Statement

I am ready.

She is cheerful.

They are friendly.

person → am/is/are → rest

− Negative

I am not ready.

She isn't cheerful.

They aren't friendly.

am/is/are + not

? Question

Am I ready?

Is she cheerful?

Are they friendly?

am/is/are goes first

Short answers

QuestionYes 👍No 👎
Are you ready?Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
Is your backpack new?Yes, it is.No, it isn't.
Are your sisters at home?Yes, they are.No, they aren't.
💡 In a short "Yes" answer we never use the short form: Yes, I am — not "Yes, I'm". In a "No" answer the short form is fine: No, I'm not.
В коротком ответе «Yes» сокращать нельзя: Yes, I am — не «Yes, I'm». А в ответе «No» сокращение возможно: No, I'm not.

🎧 Lily's new school year

Listen to the story (about one minute) and answer five questions.

00:00
Hi! My name is Lily and I am twelve years old. It is autumn now, and it is my first month at a new online school. My classroom is my laptop, my teacher is on the screen, and my classmates are from different cities. They are friendly and very cheerful, so our mornings are fun. My backpack is not heavy this year, because all my books are in the computer. My old tent is in the garage now. Summer camp is over, but it is a good memory. At the weekend my family is always outside. We are on a long hike in the forest with our two dogs. They are never lazy! My music lessons are on Tuesday. My teacher says I am talented, and after every lesson I am over the moon. A new school year is a big adventure, and I am ready for it.

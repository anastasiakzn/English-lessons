        Present Simple: look at the rules first, then four new types of exercises. You can come back to the rules at any time.

        When do we use the Present Simple?

          Scheme 1 — the verb to be

          Scheme 2 — all other verbs

          Spelling: he / she / it + -s

          RuleExamples
          Most verbs: + swork → works, live → lives, play → plays
          -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: + eswatch → watches, finish → finishes, go → goes, do → does
          consonant + y: y → iesstudy → studies, try → tries
          Irregularhave → has

          Short answers

          QuestionShort answer
          Do you / they …?Yes, I do. · No, they don't.
          Does he / she / it …?Yes, she does. · No, he doesn't.
          Are you …?Yes, I am. · No, I'm not.
          Is he / she / it …?Yes, it is. · No, it isn't.
          Are they …?Yes, they are. · No, they aren't.
          Answer with the same verb as the question: Do you…? → Yes, I do. Are you…? → Yes, I am.

          Question words

          Where …? — placeWhere does she live?
          What time / When …? — timeWhat time does the lecture start?
          What …? — thingWhat does he study?
          How often …? — frequencyHow often do you watch TV series?
          How many …? — numberHow many siblings do you have?
          How …? — way, transportHow do you get to university?
          Word order: question word + do / does + subject + verb. With to be: question word + am / is / are + subject — Where are your parents?

                  Task card

                  Describe a TV series you enjoy watching.

                  You should say:

                  • what it is called and what it is about
                  • when and how often you watch it
                  • who you watch it with

                  and explain why you like it.

                  1:00

                  Useful phrases

                  • Start: I'd like to talk about a series called… · It's a comedy / a drama / a detective series.
                  • What it is about: It's about… · The main character is… · It takes place in…
                  • When and how often: I usually watch one episode in the evening. · I watch it twice a week.
                  • Who with: I usually watch it alone / with my friend / with my sister on video call.
                  • Why: I like it because… · The actors are great. · It helps me relax after lectures.
                  One series, two series: the word is the same. My favourite series is… (not are).

Speaking

Describe a person you know who works very hard.

You should say:

  • who this person is
  • what their job is and what they usually do every day
  • what they are doing at the moment, these days

and explain why you think this person works so hard.

This is like Part 2 of the IELTS Speaking test. One minute to prepare (write key words only), then speak for 1–2 minutes.

Listening

🎧 A strange Thursday

Listen to the story (about one minute) and answer five questions.

Послушай историю (около одной минуты) и ответь на пять вопросов.
00:00
Hi! My name is Maya and I am twelve. Today is Thursday and it is a strange day in our house. My mum is under the weather, so she is at home with a hot tea and a big blanket. My dad is not at home — he is at work until six. My little brother is bored, because the weather is terrible and the park is not an option today. He is in the kitchen with my old toy cars. I am a little hungry, but the fridge is almost empty: only two apples and some cheese are there. My guitar lesson is at four o'clock, and it is half past three now, so I am not late — I am early for once! My dog is not bored at all. He is on my bed, under my blanket, and he is very happy there. Am I happy too? Well, my family is at home, my dog is warm and my lesson is in thirty minutes. Yes, I am sure: it is a good day.

1. Why is Maya's mum at home?

2. Where is Maya's dad?

3. Why is her little brother bored?

4. Is Maya late for her guitar lesson?

5. Where is her dog?

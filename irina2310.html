Warm-up: Fact of the Day

DID YOU KNOW?
1994

The first secure online purchase happened on 11 August 1994. A man in Philadelphia, USA, bought a CD by Sting on a website called NetMarket. He paid $12.48 plus delivery with his credit card.

Today people around the world spend trillions of dollars online every year.

Listening Practice (2 Exercises)

Lena's Crazy Month

💡 Listen to Lena, a marketplace seller. She is talking about her money and one strange meeting.

Sentence 0 / 16
This month my expenses were crazy. First, my best friend asked me to be the godmother of her baby son. Of course I said yes! But a godmother has to give a present, so I spent five thousand roubles on a silver spoon. Then there was the marketplace. They raised the fee for sellers again: now I pay seven percent instead of five. When I counted my money at the end of the week, my hands were shaking. On Saturday I was walking past a café when I suddenly recognised a man in the window. It was my old boss, the one who had fired me three years ago! If I were a bold person, I would have gone in and said hello. But I'm not fearless at all, so I just walked past. The funny thing is, if he hadn't fired me, I wouldn't have my own business now. So maybe I should thank him! In the evening my friend called: the christening party is next Sunday, and the restaurant costs two thousand roubles per guest. I nearly fainted! But anyway, I already love that little boy.

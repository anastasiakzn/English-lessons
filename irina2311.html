Warm-up: Idiom of the Day

Phrase of the day
To call it a night
“It's almost 3 a.m. — let's call it a night!”
Meaning: to stop what you're doing for the evening and go home or go to bed.
Закругляться, заканчивать вечер и идти домой (или спать).

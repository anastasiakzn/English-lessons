Grammar: action or non-action?

Action verbs show actions: work, watch, cook, run. They can be in the Present Continuous: I'm watching a TV series now.

Non-action verbs show feelings, thoughts and states. We usually use them only in the Present Simple, even when we talk about now.

Feelingslike, love, hate, want, need, prefer
Thinkingknow, understand, believe, remember, forget, mean
Havinghave (= own), own, belong
Otherseem, cost, depend

Verbs with two meanings

Some verbs can be action or non-action. It depends on the meaning.

VerbNon-action → only Present SimpleAction → Present Continuous is OK
haveиметь: I have a car / a headache / a day off.в выражениях: have lunch, a shower, a meeting, fun → I'm having lunch.
thinkсчитать, полагать: I think it's a good idea.думать, размышлять: I'm thinking about my holiday.
tasteиметь вкус: This soup tastes great.пробовать на вкус: The chef is tasting the soup.

lookвыглядеть: You look tired.смотреть: I'm looking at the photos.

feel (чувствовать себя): both are OK — I feel tired = I'm feeling tired.

Useful phrases

  • I'd like to talk about my … / The person I want to describe is …

  • At the weekend she normally …
  • At the moment he's working on … / These days she's spending a lot of time …
  • He doesn't have much time for himself, so his mood …
  • I think he needs some space to relax.

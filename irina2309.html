Reading Practice

The Board Game Night

💡 Read the story, then choose the correct answer to each question. Прочитайте историю и выберите правильный ответ на каждый вопрос.

Kate sells board games and puzzles on a big marketplace. Last Saturday, she invited her friends to her country house for a board game night. She wanted to test a new game before she added it to her store. The rules were simple: you roll the dice, move your piece and solve a small puzzle. Everyone had to memorise the rules, but after two glasses of wine nobody could remember a thing.

Kate's friend Olga is like a child at parties: she loves to win and hates to lose. When she rolled the dice for the tenth time and still got a one, she said the dice were broken. The pieces had a funny shape — they looked like tiny bottles of prosecco. "This game reminds me of our trip to Suzdal!" Olga laughed.

The next morning, Kate read online reviews of the game from another seller. Many buyers were unhappy. The boxes had arrived broken because the courier had used too much force. Even worse, the seller had dropped the ball: nobody answered the customers' questions for a week. "Good interaction with customers is everything," Kate thought. "If he had answered their messages, he wouldn't have got so many bad reviews."

In the end, Kate decided to roll the dice and add the game to her store — with stronger boxes and fast replies. "If we hadn't played it last night, I would never have found its problems," she told Olga.

Practice: Third Conditional (Условные предложения 3-го типа)

Grammar Theory

We use the Third Conditional to talk about impossible situations in the past. We imagine how things could have been different if a past action had not happened.

If-clause (Условие в прошлом) Main clause (Результат в прошлом)
If + Past Perfect (had + V3) would have + V3
Example: If he had worked harder, the CEO would not have fired him.
(Если бы он работал усерднее, директор бы его не уволил - но в реальности он работал плохо и его уволили).
Example: We would have received the document if she had sent the email.

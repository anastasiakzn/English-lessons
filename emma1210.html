Grammar

⭐ Present Simple

The verb to be is done. Now we take all the other verbs: work, live, go, read, wake up…

When do we use the Present Simple?
01

Regular actions and facts in general

I live in St Petersburg. My brother studies abroad.
Регулярные действия и факты вообще — то, что верно не только сейчас, а в целом: «я живу в Санкт-Петербурге», «мой брат учится за границей».
02

Repeated actions

Indicators: every day · usually · often · sometimes · seldom · never · twice a week · on Mondays
I play the guitar twice a week. My sister often reads in the evening.
Повторяющиеся действия. Слова-подсказки: every day — каждый день, usually — обычно, often — часто, sometimes — иногда, seldom — редко, never — никогда, twice a week — дважды в неделю, on Mondays — по понедельникам.
03

Scientific facts

Water boils at 100 degrees. The sun rises in the east.
Научные факты и законы природы: вода кипит при 100 градусах, солнце встаёт на востоке.
04

Timetables — films, performances, flights, trains, buses

Our English lesson starts at four. The film starts at eight tonight.
Расписание: уроки, сеансы, спектакли, рейсы, поезда, автобусы. «Урок начинается в четыре», «фильм начинается в восемь» — даже если речь о завтрашнем дне.
Scheme 2 — all other verbs
+ Affirmative
I / We / You / They + V1
I play the guitar every day.
He / She / It + V1 + s
She plays the guitar every day.
− Negative
I / We / You / They + don't + V1
I don't play football on Sunday.
He / She / It + doesn't + V1
He doesn't play football on Sunday.
? Question
Do + I / we / you / they + V1 …?
Do you play football on Sunday?
Does + he / she / it + V1 …?
Does he play football on Sunday?
V1 is the first form of the verb — the infinitive without to: to play → play, to go → go.
Схема для всех глаголов, кроме to be:
  • Утверждение: подлежащее + V1. Для he / she / it к глаголу добавляется -s: I play → She plays.
  • Отрицание: подлежащее + don't (для he / she / it — doesn't) + V1. Окончание -s уже «ушло» в doesn't, поэтому сам глагол остаётся без -s: He doesn't play, а не «doesn't plays».
  • Вопрос: Do (для he / she / it — Does) + подлежащее + V1. Снова без -s: Does he play?
  • Короткие ответы: Yes, I do. / No, I don't. Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
  • V1 — первая форма глагола, инфинитив без частицы to: to play → play.
  • Написание -s: play → plays, read → reads; go → goes, watch → watches; study → studies, cry → cries.
  • Важно: глаголу to be помощники do / does не нужны: «I am tired», «Is she ill?» — а не «I do be tired».

📖 Two very different mornings

Read the text and do the two tasks below.

Прочитай текст и выполни два задания ниже.

Hi! We are Vera and Polina. We are cousins, we are both twelve and we are in the same boat: school, homework and music lessons every day. But our mornings are very different.

Vera wakes up at six o'clock. She is always early. She makes her bed, she puts her blanket in the cupboard and she reads a book before school. She likes quiet mornings and she doesn't like loud music.

Polina wakes up at half past seven and she is often late. Her blanket is on the floor, her books are under the bed and she listens to loud music in the bathroom. "Morning sounds are my favourite sounds!" she says. Her mum is not sure about that.

This week Polina is ill. She is in bed with a hot tea and two blankets, and the house is very quiet. Vera is under the weather too, but only a little. So the cousins are in the same boat again: no school, no music lessons, only films and tea.

"Do you like our quiet week?" Vera asks. "No, I don't!" Polina answers. "I want my loud mornings back!"

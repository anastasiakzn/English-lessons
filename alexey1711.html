Grammar: Past Simple

We use the Past Simple for finished actions at a finished time in the past.

Signal words: yesterday, last Monday / week / year, two days ago, in 2015, at eleven o'clock, then.

+ STATEMENTsubject + 2nd formWe got to the hotel late.
They described the problem.
− NEGATIVEsubject + didn't + 1st formHe didn't find out the truth.
We didn't go home early.
? QUESTION(question word) + did + subject + 1st formWhen did you get there?
Did they damage the fence?

After did and didn't the verb goes back to the 1st form: didn't find, not didn't found.  Did you go home?, not Did you went home?

Regular verbs: + ed

RuleExamples
most verbs: + edask → asked  ·  look → looked  ·  search → searched  ·  consider → considered
verb ending in -e: + ddamage → damaged  ·  describe → described  ·  decide → decided
consonant + y → iedcarry → carried  ·  try → tried  ·  study → studied
short verb, one vowel: double the consonantstop → stopped  ·  plan → planned

Irregular verbs for this lesson

be → was / wereget → gotfind → found go → wenthang → hungcome → came say → saidsee → sawtake → took think → thoughthave → hadlose → lost break → brokebegin → beganmake → made do → didput → putleave → left

Reading: an inspection in the mountains

A. Last October I travelled to the mountains to inspect the construction of a small boutique hotel. I got my driving license ten years ago, so I rented a car at the airport and drove there myself. The mountain road was narrow and dangerous, and at some moments I was a little scared of the height. Unfortunately, heavy rain started in the afternoon, so I got to the hotel two hours later than planned.

B. At the entrance, I met the subcontractors and looked at their work. In addition to the main building, we wanted them to build a high fence around the parking area. They described their progress and showed me the plans that hung on the wall in the site office. At the same time, I noticed a problem: a delivery truck hit the gate in the morning and damaged a section of the new fence.

C. We searched for extra metal panels in the storage room, but we didn't find any. I asked the local supplier for an urgent delivery, and we considered two different solutions. In the evening, I finally found out that the new panels were already on the way. I didn't go home that night — instead, I went to my colleague's place in a nearby town, and the next day we finished the inspection successfully.

Warm-up: Work Phrase of the Day

To burn the midnight oil
"We were so snowed under with orders that we had to burn the midnight oil to finish everything."
Meaning: To work, study, or read late into the night.
(Засиживаться допоздна, работать до глубокой ночи)

Question for Discussion:

When was the last time you had to burn the midnight oil because you were snowed under with tasks? How did you feel the next day?

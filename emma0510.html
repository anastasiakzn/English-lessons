Grammar

⭐ The verb to be

PersonFull formShort formExample
IamI'mI am under the weather.
youareyou'reYou are near the window.
he / she / itishe's / she's / it'sMy cat is under the table.
wearewe'reWe are at home.
theyarethey'reMy books are behind the door.

+ Statement

I am late.

The weather is terrible.

My pencils are there.

person → am/is/are → rest

− Negative

I am not late.

The weather isn't terrible.

My pencils aren't there.

am/is/are + not

? Question

Am I late?

Is the weather terrible?

Are your pencils there?

am/is/are goes first

QuestionYes 👍No 👎
Are you at home?Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
Is your cat under the bed?Yes, it is.No, it isn't.
Are your books there?Yes, they are.No, they aren't.
💡 The word order in a question never changes: Is your room tidy? — not "Tidy is your room?"
Порядок слов в вопросе фиксированный: Is your room tidy? Нельзя начинать вопрос с прилагательного или с подлежащего.

Reading

📖 Nina's messy room

Read the text and do the two tasks below.

Прочитай текст и выполни два задания ниже.

Hello! I am Nina and I am twelve. Our house is near the river, so the view from my window is amazing. My room is upstairs, next to my parents' room.

Today the weather is terrible. It is only three degrees, it is windy and the sky is grey. My umbrella is behind the door, but it is not useful today — I am at home with my little brother.

My room is a bit of a mess. My books are not on the shelf — they are under my bed. My school bag is behind the chair, and my pencils are between the lamp and my laptop. Only my guitar is in its place: it is next to the window, in a black bag.

My brother is not in his room. He is here, on my floor, with his toy cars. For him my messy room is a perfect place. And for me? Well, it is a mess, but it is my mess — and it is cosy.

Grammar: Past Simple

We use the Past Simple for completed actions in the past.

Indicators (Signal words): yesterday, last week, last month, in 2015, two days ago, when.

+ PositiveSubject + verb in the 2nd form

We dug the foundation pit.
He considered the plan.
− NegativeSubject + did + not + verb in the 1st form

They did not search for the tire.
I didn't ask him.
? QuestionQuestion word + did + subject + verb in the 1st form

Why did you wait here?
When did the flood happen?

Reading: A Challenging Project in the Mountains

Last year, our team went to the mountains to build a new tourist base. The location was beautiful but very challenging. First, we had to search for a good space for the foundation pit, but the area was full of rocks. We considered several options and finally made a decision to build near the river.

However, it was quite dangerous. After two weeks of work, heavy rain started, and there was a real danger of a flood. The water went over the temporary fence, and we had to stop all operations. The manager asked us for extra help to secure the equipment. We waited for three days until the weather became good again. It was a really tough experience, but we successfully finished the entrance and the main structure.

Speaking

Describe a challenging project or task you finished in the past.

You should say:

  • what the project or task was
  • where and when you did it
  • what problems or dangers you faced

and explain how you solved the problems and finished it.

This is Part 2 of the IELTS Speaking test. Make short notes for one minute, then speak for one to two minutes. Focus on using the Past Simple accurately.

В прошлом году наша компания работала над новым отелем, и это был действительно сложный проект. EN Last year our company worked on a new hotel, and it was a really challenging project.
Мы хотели, чтобы рабочие вырыли большой котлован, но в том районе не было свободного пространства. EN We wanted the workers to dig a big foundation pit, but there was no free space in that area.
Кроме того, мы узнали о реальной опасности наводнения. EN In addition, we found out about a real danger of a flood.
Я попросил менеджера о помощи, и мы рассмотрели другое решение. EN I asked the manager for help, and we considered another decision.
Мы искали хорошие материалы и построили высокое ограждение над входом. EN We searched for good materials and built a tall fence over the entrance.
Это было очень опасно, поэтому мы ждали там несколько дней. EN It was very dangerous, so we waited there for a few days.
В итоге высота воды не разрушила нашу работу, и мы успешно закончили стройку. EN In the end, the height of the water didn't ruin our work, and we finished the construction successfully.
